Jungro Lee

MSc Machine Learning · University College London  ·  jungro7982@gmail.com

I am a Master's student in Machine Learning at University College London (UCL), working at the intersection of deep learning and cancer biology. My research focuses on developing machine learning methods for multimodal biomedical data — spanning medical image analysis, graph neural networks on cell spatial graphs, and generative models for genomics.

My current dissertation project applies graph neural networks to cell spatial graphs derived from histopathology slides (TCGA-BRCA), integrating mRNA expression data to predict breast cancer recurrence. I am broadly interested in how generative and causal models can advance mechanistic understanding of disease and treatment response at the single-cell level.

Research Interests
  • Graph Neural Networks for spatial cell analysis and cancer genomics
  • Generative and causal models for single-cell transcriptomics
  • Multimodal learning for cancer biology (imaging + genomics)
  • Deep learning for medical image segmentation (CT/MRI/WSI)
